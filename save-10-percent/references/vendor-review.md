# Ranked vendor review

Review one liability at a time, highest plausible net saving first.

## Vendor card

Show:

- provider, account alias, cadence, typical charge, annualized cost, and confidence;
- current plan, renewal, and contract evidence when available;
- current usage state and dependent people or workflows;
- the next three to five grouped questions.

Ask:

1. Who uses this, for which required outcome, and when was it last meaningfully used?
2. Which seats, data, assets, automations, customers, staff, or family members depend on it?
3. Which features are genuinely required?
4. What renewal, notice, promotion, bundle, or retention condition applies?
5. Would cancellation, pause, downgrade, consolidation, negotiation, or replacement preserve the outcome?

Challenge “might need it” by asking for the concrete next use and consequence of losing access. Challenge “cancel it” by checking data, ownership, integrations, and other users. Never override a protected outcome.

## Alternatives

Compare an alternative only when it preserves required outcomes. Use current official pricing and feature documentation. Record:

- recurring replacement price;
- setup and exit fees;
- lost bundle value;
- migration hours and known monetary cost;
- required data transfer;
- reliability, privacy, lock-in, and rollback;
- promotional price expiry.

Do not recommend switching when net 12-month savings are non-positive, core features are missing, or migration evidence is incomplete.

## Exhaustion

A liability is complete only when it is kept, included in the candidate batch, or blocked with the exact missing evidence. Continue through all in-scope liabilities when the candidate total remains below 10%.
