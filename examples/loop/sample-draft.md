# Sample draft

A made-up product update email for a made-up notes app. It is weak on purpose.

```text
Subject: Exciting updates!

Hi there,

We are thrilled to announce that we have been working really hard on a lot of
improvements to Acme Notes. There are many new things that we think you will
love, and the team is very excited about what is coming next.

Search is better now. We also fixed some bugs. More details soon!

Let us know what you think.

Best,
The Acme Notes Team
```

Facts the email may use (all invented for this example):

- Search now looks inside PDF attachments.
- Search results show in under one second on a 5,000-note library in the team's own test.
- The sync bug that duplicated notes on a second device is fixed.
- The update is free for every plan and is already live.

Try it with:

```text
Use $loop on the email in examples/loop/sample-draft.md. Make it 100/100 for a busy customer who skims. Use only the facts listed under the draft. Do not send anything.
```

What to expect: a rubric of 5 to 8 criteria that adds up to 100, a starting score with every lost point named, one small revision per loop with a new score, and a final scorecard. If the loop cannot honestly reach 100, it stops as `blocked_below_100` and says which points are missing and why.
