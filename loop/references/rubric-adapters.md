# Rubric Adapters

Use these as starters, then adapt to the actual session. Every final rubric must total exactly 100 points.

## Thumbnail / Visual

- Mobile legibility and instant read.
- Visual hook, curiosity, and title-thumbnail fit.
- Likeness or identity fidelity when real people appear.
- Composition, contrast, hierarchy, and uncluttered focus.
- Technical cleanliness: artifacts, cropping, brand marks, reference usage.
- Hard-veto candidates: unreadable at mobile size, wrong person, misleading entity, amateur composition, visible forbidden brand mark.

## Video / Edit

- First 5-10 seconds and promise clarity.
- Pacing, dead-air removal, continuity, and story payoff.
- Audio intelligibility and visual clarity.
- Caption and subtitle readability and placement.
- Retention moments, pattern breaks, and CTA timing.
- Hard-veto candidates: broken audio, incoherent story, unreadable captions, export defects, missing required asset.

## Writing / Draft

- Hook strength and reader promise.
- Fit with the author's voice or the channel.
- Specificity, proof, and useful insight.
- Structure, rhythm, and compression.
- CTA or next-step fit.
- No AI tells, placeholders, unsupported claims, or generic filler.
- Hard-veto candidates: wrong facts, fake proof, off-voice salesy copy, live-action claims not authorized.

## Social / Carousel / Thread

- Scroll-stop opening.
- Saveability, shareability, and audience fit.
- Sequence logic and payoff.
- Copy-visual pairing.
- Platform-native formatting.
- Hard-veto candidates: unreadable slides, weak first slide, unsupported stats, generic listicle shape.

## Pipeline / QA / Generic

- Source truth coverage.
- Required artifact completeness.
- Validator and check pass state.
- Proof-state accuracy.
- Edge cases and regression risk.
- Cleanup and final report quality.
- Hard-veto candidates: stale source, missing required proof, unsafe live action, failed validator, ambiguous production impact.
